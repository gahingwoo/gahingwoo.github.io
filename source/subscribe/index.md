This blog has an [RSS/Atom feed](/atom.xml) — new posts show up in your feed
reader automatically, no account or email needed.

To subscribe, copy this URL into a feed reader (Feedly, Inoreader,
NetNewsWire, Reeder, etc.):

```
https://gahingwoo.github.io/atom.xml
```

If you opened this page from the RSS icon expecting something to happen
automatically: it doesn't, on purpose — feed URLs are meant to be pasted into
a reader, not clicked in a browser. Opening [the feed itself](/atom.xml)
directly just shows you the raw XML, which is normal and is what the reader
software actually consumes.
